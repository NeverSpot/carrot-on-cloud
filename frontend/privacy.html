Privacy Policy for Carrot on Cloud

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Carrot on Cloud is a browser extension designed to provide real-time rating predictions for Codeforces contests.

1. Data Collection

The extension collects the following data from your current browser tab when you visit a Codeforces standings page:

  • Contest ID: The ID of the contest you are viewing.
  • User Handles: The handles of participants currently visible in the standings table.

2. How Data is Used

The collected data is sent to our backend (hosted on Supabase) to:

  • Fetch current ratings and calculate expected rating changes using the FFT-based algorithm.
  • Cache results to reduce the load on Codeforces servers.

3. Data Storage & Privacy

  • We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not store your browser history, cookies, or any other personal data.
  • Collected handles and contest IDs are stored temporarily in our database to provide fast results to all users.

4. Third-Party Services

Our backend is hosted on Supabase. We do not sell or share data with any third-party advertisers or services.

5. Contact

For any questions, please visit our GitHub repository: github.com/NeverSpot/carrot-on-cloud
